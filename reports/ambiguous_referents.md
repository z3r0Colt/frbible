# Divine pronouns left unchanged on purpose

During the New Testament review these places were left as the PDF colored them because Reformed interpreters differ over whether the pronoun means the Father, the Son or the Spirit (or a man). They are the places to look at if you want to make a call of your own.

## romans galatians

- Romans 8:37	him (GF) "him that loved us": Christ (love of Christ, 8:35) or God (love of God, 8:39); left GF
- Romans 10:12	him (GF) "call upon him": "the same Lord over all" is likely Christ (10:9,13) but Lord/him Father-vs-Son is debatable; left GF
- Romans 10:14	him#1, him#2 (GF) "call on him... believe in him": same Lord of 10:12-13, likely Christ but debatable; left GF (the whom's, wrongly PR, changed to GF to match)
- Romans 15:9	I (PR) "I will confess to thee among the Gentiles" (Ps 18:49): David, or Christ speaking through David; left PR
- Romans 15:10	he (PR) "again he saith": God, the scripture, or Moses as speaker; left PR
- Romans 10:21	he (PR) "to Israel he saith": Esaias (10:20) most natural, though God is the speaker of the quote; left PR
- Galatians 1:5	whom (GF) "to whom be glory": God the Father (1:4b) or Christ (1:3-4a); left GF
- Galatians 3:5	He / he (PR) "He that ministereth to you the Spirit": God (most) or Paul/ministers (some); left PR
- Galatians 1:6	him (GF) "him that called you": Father (usual Reformed reading) though some say Christ; left GF
- Galatians 5:8	him (GF) "him that calleth you": God the Father most likely; left GF

## corinthians

- 1 Corinthians 1:8	who (PR#1) — clearly divine, so changed PR->GOD_FATHER, but the antecedent is debated: Christ (nearest, v7) or God (v4, v9 "God is faithful"; Calvin/Hodge). Review if the Son reading is preferred.
- 1 Corinthians 2:16	him (GF#1) — "the Lord" of Isa 40:13 (YHWH), but Paul pairs it with "the mind of Christ"; some read the Lord as Christ. Left GF.
- 1 Corinthians 4:7	who (PR#1) — rhetorical "who maketh thee to differ?" (implied answer God), but it is an interrogative, not a reference. Left PR.
- 1 Corinthians 10:22	he (GF#1) — "are we stronger than he?" The Lord here may be Christ (cup/table of the Lord, v21) or YHWH (Deut 32 echo). Left GF.
- 1 Corinthians 15:25	he (GF#2) — "till he hath put all enemies under his feet": subject may be Christ or the Father (Ps 110:1, cf. v27). Left GF; he#1 and his#1 changed to GS.
- 1 Corinthians 15:27	he (GF#2) — "when he saith": God speaking in Ps 8 or Scripture generically. Left GF (he#1, he#3 are clearly the Father).
- 2 Corinthians 2:14	his (GF#1) — "savour of his knowledge": God's (subject of the clause) or Christ's (nearest noun). Left GF.
- 2 Corinthians 5:9	him (GF#1) — "accepted of him": the Lord of v8 (likely Christ, cf. v10) or God. Left GF.
- 2 Corinthians 6:1	him (GF#1) — italic supplied word; "workers together with him" most likely God (5:20). Left GF.
- 2 Corinthians 6:2	thee (PR#1, PR#2) — Isa 49:8 addressed to the Servant (Messiah), but Paul applies it to the present day of salvation for believers. Left PR.
- 2 Corinthians 9:9	He/he/his (GF) — Ps 112:9; in the Psalm the subject is the righteous generous man (Calvin, Hodge), but some read God's righteousness here. Left GF.

## eph phil col

- Ephesians 1:7	his#2 (GF) "riches of his grace": Father's grace (as 1:6) or Christ's (nearest antecedent); left GF
- Ephesians 1:17	him (GF) "knowledge of him": most read the Father, some Christ; left GF
- Ephesians 3:5	his (GF) "his holy apostles": God's or Christ's (mystery of Christ, 3:4); left GF
- Ephesians 5:14	he (PR) "Wherefore he saith": speaker is Scripture/God or an early hymn; not clearly a human or divine person; left PR
- Ephesians 6:10	his (GF) "power of his might": "the Lord" may be Christ or God; left GF
- Colossians 1:20	himself (GF) "reconcile all things unto himself": Father (Calvin, most) vs Christ; left GF
- Colossians 1:21	he (GF) "hath he reconciled": Father (continuing 1:19-20) or Christ (body of his flesh, 1:22); left GF
- Colossians 1:22	his#2 (GF) "in his sight": Father or Christ as the one presenting; left GF
- Colossians 1:29	his (GF) "his working": Christ (1:28) or God; left GF
- Colossians 2:14	his (GF) "nailing it to his cross": subject God vs Christ debated in 2:13-15; cross is Christ's but pronoun follows the subject; left GF
- Colossians 2:15	he (GF) "he made a shew of them": subject God (2:13) vs Christ debated; left GF
- Colossians 3:10	him (GF) "image of him that created him": God the Creator (usual) or Christ; left GF

## hebrews

- Hebrews 2:6-8	him/his (PR) in Ps 8: man generally vs Christ as the true Man (2:9); left PR. "he" (GF) in 2:8 = God, kept.
- Hebrews 3:3	he/who (PR) "he who hath builded the house": a general maxim vs Christ the builder (Owen) vs God (3:4); left PR.
- Hebrews 3:4	he (GF) "he that built all things is God": God generally vs Christ as builder (Owen); left GF.
- Hebrews 3:6	his (GF) "over his own house": God's house (as 3:2,5) vs Christ's own house (KJV "own"); left GF. whose (PR->GF in tsv) follows this choice.
- Hebrews 3:7-11	me/my/I (GF) in Ps 95 quoted "as the Holy Ghost saith": speaker the LORD (GF) vs Holy Spirit vs Christ tempted in the wilderness (1 Cor 10:9); left GF.
- Hebrews 4:4,4:7,4:8	he (GF) "he spake / he limiteth / would he have spoken": God speaking in Scripture vs the Holy Spirit; left GF.
- Hebrews 4:10	he/his #1-2 (PR) "he that is entered into his rest": believer (most) vs Christ entering his rest (Owen); left PR. his#3 (God's works) fixed to GF.
- Hebrews 4:13	his/him/whom (GF) "in his sight... with whom we have to do": God vs the Word/Christ (from 4:12); left GF.
- Hebrews 5:11	whom (PR) "Of whom we have many things to say": Melchisedec vs Christ vs neuter "which matter"; left PR.
- Hebrews 7:27	his (PR) "first for his own sins": the Levitical high priests' sins (generic) - grammatically tied to Christ's hypothetical offering; left PR.
- Hebrews 10:15-17	I/my (GF) in Jer 31 quote introduced as the Holy Ghost's witness: "saith the LORD" keeps it GF; could be argued HS; left GF. ("he had said" in 10:15 fixed to HS.)
- Hebrews 11:28	he (PR#2) "he that destroyed the firstborn": the destroying angel vs the LORD (Exod 12:23,29); left PR.
- Hebrews 12:25	him#1/#2 (GF) and him (PR) "him that spake on earth": God vs Christ speaking from heaven; "on earth" = Moses vs God at Sinai; left as is.
- Hebrews 12:26	whose/he/I (GF) "whose voice then shook the earth": God at Sinai vs the Son (Owen); left GF.
- Hebrews 13:21	whom (GF) "to whom be glory": God of peace (13:20) vs Jesus Christ (nearest antecedent); interpreters split; left GF.

## general epistles

- James 5:6	he (PR) - "the just": a righteous man generally; some see Christ. Left PR.
- 1 Peter 4:1	he (PR) - "he that hath suffered in the flesh": generic believer vs Christ. Left PR.
- 1 Peter 4:5	him (GF) - judge of quick and dead: Father (1:17) or Christ (Acts 10:42). Left GF.
- 1 Peter 4:11	whom (GF) - doxology: to God or to Jesus Christ (nearest). Left GF.
- 1 Peter 4:14	he x2 (GF) - "he is evil spoken of / glorified": Spirit of glory vs Christ vs God. Left GF.
- 2 Peter 1:3	his, him (GF) - divine power / him that called us: Christ (1:2 Jesus our Lord) or God. Left GF.
- 2 Peter 3:9	his (GF) - the Lord's promise: Father or Christ. Left GF.
- 2 Peter 3:13	his (GF) - his promise: after "day of God" (3:12); Father or Christ. Left GF.
- 2 Peter 3:14	him (GF) - found of him in peace: God or Christ as judge. Left GF.
- 1 John 1:5	him #1 (GF) - message heard of him: Christ (apostolic hearing) or God. Left GF.
- 1 John 2:3-5	him, his (GF) - know him / his commandments / his word / in him: Father or Son. Left GF.
- 1 John 2:6	him (GF) - abideth in him: Father or Son (only "as he walked" changed). Left GF.
- 1 John 2:8	him (GF) - true in him: likely Christ, not certain. Left GF.
- 1 John 2:12	his (GF) - his name's sake: Christ or God. Left GF.
- 1 John 2:25	he - changed PR->GF (default divine) only; Father vs Son debated.
- 1 John 2:27	him x2 (GF) - anointing received of him / abide in him: Father, Son (or Holy One 2:20). Left GF.
- 1 John 2:28	him #1 (GF) - abide in him: Father or Son (appearing/coming pronouns changed to GS).
- 1 John 2:29	he, him (GF) - he is righteous / born of him: shifts to God (born of God) vs Christ. Left GF.
- 1 John 3:1	him (GF) - world knew him not: Father or Christ. Left GF.
- 1 John 3:2	he, him x2, he (GF) - when he (or "it") shall appear, like him, see him: Father vs Son debated. Left GF.
- 1 John 3:3	him - changed PR->GF (default divine) only; Father vs Son debated.
- 1 John 3:6	him x3 (GF) - abideth in him / seen him / known him: probably Christ (3:5), not certain. Left GF.
- 1 John 3:23	he (GF) - "as he gave us commandment": Father or Christ (John 13:34). Left GF.
- 1 John 3:24	his, him, he (GF) - keepeth his commandments, dwelleth in him: God vs Christ. Left GF.
- 1 John 4:4	he #1 (GF) - greater is he that is in you: God, Spirit, or Christ. Left GF.
- 1 John 4:21	him (GF) - commandment from him: God or Christ. Left GF.
- 1 John 5:1	him #1 (PR) - "him also that is begotten of him": fellow believer (majority) vs Christ. Left PR.
- 1 John 5:14-15	him, his, he (GF) - confidence in him / ask according to his will: Father or Son (5:13). Left GF.
- 1 John 5:16	he #1 (GF) - "he shall give him life": God vs the praying brother. Left GF.
- 1 John 5:18	he, himself (PR) - "he that is begotten of God keepeth himself": believer (TR) vs Christ. Left PR.
- 1 John 5:20	him x2, his (GF) - him that is true: Father (with "his Son"); some read Christ. Left GF.
- Jude 1:6	he (GF) - antecedent "the Lord" (v.5), some MSS read Jesus. Left GF.

## acts thess pastorals

- Acts 1:24	Thou/thou (GS) - prayer "Lord, which knowest the hearts": Christ (chose the apostles) vs Father (cf. 15:8); interpreters split; left GS
- Acts 5:32	his (GS) - "we are his witnesses": God (5:31 subject) vs Christ (exalted Prince); left GS (whom/him in same verse fixed)
- Acts 9:5	Who (PR) - interrogative "Who art thou, Lord?" (also 22:8, 26:15): question form, speaker does not yet know referent; left PR
- Acts 13:35	he (PR) - "he saith also in another psalm": God (continuing 13:34) vs David the psalmist; left PR (Thou/thine fixed to GF)
- Acts 13:47	I (GS) - "the Lord commanded us, saying, I have set thee": Isa 49:6 speaker is the Father, but Paul's commission came from Christ (cf. 26:17-18); left GS
- Acts 14:3	his (GS) - "the Lord, which gave testimony unto the word of his grace": Lord = Christ vs God (cf. 20:32); left GS
- Acts 18:10	I (GS) - Lord in the night vision; usually taken as Christ (cf. 23:11), but not certain; left GS
- Acts 20:28	he/his (GS) - "church of God, which he hath purchased with his own blood": God (communicatio idiomatum) vs Christ; GS defensible; left
- Acts 22:15	his (GS) - "thou shalt be his witness": the Just One (Christ, nearest) vs God of our fathers (subject of 22:14); left GS
- Acts 28:27	I (PR) - "I should heal them" (Isa 6:10): the LORD speaking, but 28:25 attributes the words to the Holy Ghost; GF vs HS; left PR
- 1 Thessalonians 3:13	he (GF) - "to the end he may stablish your hearts": the Lord of 3:12 (likely Christ) vs God; left GF (his saints fixed to GS)
- 2 Thessalonians 2:7	he/who (PR) - "he who now letteth": the restrainer; identity disputed (Roman state, Holy Spirit, etc.); left PR
- 2 Thessalonians 3:3	who (GF) - "the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you": Lord = Christ (Pauline usage) vs God; left GF
- 2 Thessalonians 3:16	himself (GF) - "the Lord of peace himself": Christ vs God (cf. "God of peace" 1 Thess 5:23); left GF
- 1 Timothy 6:15	his/he/who (GF) - "in his times he shall shew, who is the blessed and only Potentate": Father (majority reading) vs Christ; left GF
- 2 Timothy 1:12	whom/he/him (GF) - "I know whom I have believed": Christ vs God the Father; interpreters split; left GF
- 2 Timothy 2:19	his (GF) - "The Lord knoweth them that are his": quotation of Num 16:5 (LORD) vs Lord Jesus; left GF
- 2 Timothy 2:26	him/his (PR) - "taken captive by him at his will": the devil vs God / the Lord's servant; left PR
- 2 Timothy 4:18	his/whom (GF) - "the Lord ... his heavenly kingdom: to whom be glory": Lord likely Christ (cf. 4:1, 4:8) but doxology could be to God; left GF
- Titus 3:7	his (GF) - "justified by his grace": God our Saviour (3:4-5) vs Jesus Christ (nearest, 3:6); left GF

## gospels revelation

- Matthew 3:3 / Mark 1:3 / Luke 3:4 - "make his paths straight": his (GS) = the Lord of Isa 40:3, applied to Jesus; left GS.
- Matthew 9:38 / Luke 10:2 - "Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest": usually the Father, but Christ sends the labourers next (Mt 10:1, Lk 10:1); left GS.
- Matthew 13:15 - "and I should heal them" (Isa 6:10): speaker is God of Isa 6, but John 12:41 identifies that glory as Christ's; left GS.
- Matthew 13:35 - "I will open my mouth in parables" (Ps 78:2): GF is likely wrong, but referent split between psalmist (PR) and Christ as fulfiller (GS); left GF.
- Matthew 21:13 / Mark 11:17 / Luke 19:46 - "My house shall be called the house of prayer": God's house (Isa 56:7) but spoken by Jesus as his own; left GS.
- Luke 1:17 - "he shall go before him": him = "the Lord their God" (1:16), fulfilled in Jesus; left GS.
- Luke 1:76-77 - "prepare his ways... unto his people": his = the Lord whom John precedes (Father or Christ); left GS.
- Luke 7:29 - "all the people that heard him": him = Jesus or John the Baptist; left GS.
- Luke 11:49 - "said the wisdom of God, I will send them prophets": I = personified wisdom of God, or Christ (cf. Mt 23:34); left GS.
- John 5:32 - "another that beareth witness of me... the witness which he witnesseth": he = the Father (most) or John the Baptist; left PR.
- John 6:31 - "He gave them bread from heaven": God (Ps 78:24) vs Moses as the crowd implies (6:32); left GS.
- John 12:40 - "He hath blinded their eyes... and I should heal them": God of Isa 6 vs Christ (12:41 "his glory"); left He=GS, I=PR.
- John 13:32 - "God shall also glorify him in himself": himself = God or the Son (en auto); left GS.
- John 14:23 / 17:11 / 17:21 / 17:22 - "we/our/us" = Father and Son together; plural, left GS.
- Revelation 1:8 - "I am Alpha and Omega... saith the Lord... the Almighty": Father or Son, interpreters split; left GS.
- Revelation 6:10 - "How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge": God (Despotes) vs Christ ("holy and true" 3:7); left GS.
- Revelation 6:17 - "the great day of his wrath": the Lamb (nearest) or God and the Lamb; left GS.
- Revelation 11:15 - "and he shall reign for ever": the Lord, his Christ, or both as one; left GS.
- Revelation 12:5 - "a man child, who was to rule all nations": Christ (most) vs the church; who is PR, left.
- Revelation 14:14 - "one sat like unto the Son of man, having on his head": Christ vs an angel (cf. 14:15); his is PR, left.
- Revelation 16:16 - "And he gathered them together": the demonic spirits (16:14), or God; GS doubtful but target unclear; left GS.
- Revelation 20:11 - "him that sat on it, from whose face": great white throne - Father or Christ as judge; left GS.
- Revelation 22:3-4 - "his servants shall serve him... see his face": God and the Lamb on one throne; singular referent debated; left GS.
- Revelation 22:6 - "the Lord God of the holy prophets sent his angel": Father (cf. 1:1) vs Christ (cf. 22:16); left GS.
- Revelation 22:14 - "do his commandments": Christ (speaker 22:12-16) or God; left GS.
